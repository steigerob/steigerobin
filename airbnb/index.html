But first, coffee

For the Bosch Tassimo, check the water, pop in a T-Disc, close the lid, and press the button. Help yourself to the treats in your room, too.

Red light? The water tank probably needs a top-up.

A place at the table

You’re welcome to use the kitchen utensils and spices. Keep your food in the fridge compartment labelled “Airbnb”. Can’t find something? Let us know.

Your evening, sorted

The TV has a Fire TV Stick, and you’re welcome to use our Netflix account. Put your feet up and find something good to watch.

A little bathroom kindness

The shower gel and shampoo labelled “AirBnB” are for you to use. Forgotten your toothbrush? Ask us — we have a spare for emergencies.

Set the mood

The white remote dims the desk and floor lamps. For a darker room, simply lower the roller shutter and get comfortable.
