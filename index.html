PROFESSIONAL

Robotics Software Engineering in Practice

Hey, I'm Robin, a passionate software engineer focused on robotics, safety-relevant validation, and the interesting problems that begin when robots meet the real world.

Since joining FERCHAU at SICK AG, I have been working on safety-relevant validation scenarios for mobile robots, connecting software, robot control, and precise pose tracking in real test environments.

My work combines ROS 2, C++, Python, UR and AGV robot control, LidarLoc, and RTDE. Before that, my master's thesis and research work took me deep into autonomous navigation, SLAM, and local planning. The results were promising enough that the work continues today: together with my university supervisors, I am preparing a paper for IEEE RA-L and IROS as lead author. Earlier roles shaped my interest in requirements engineering, WPF applications, and software that people actually use.

EXPERIENCE

Current Work & Previous Roles

A compact overview of work I have pursued with plenty of passion and curiosity.

Robotics Software Engineer (FERCHAU)Sep. 2025 - Present

I set up and execute manual, safety-relevant validation scenarios for mobile robot systems in real test environments at SICK AG. The work combines ROS 2, C++, Python, UR20 and AGV robot control, LidarLoc-based pose tracking, and RTDE communication, with a strong focus on reliable test execution and traceable validation quality.

Software Development & Research (ISR)Oct. 2023 - Oct. 2024

During my research internship in Lisbon, I developed ROS-based modules for autonomous navigation. My work focused on SLAM, local planning, and improving pose estimation for mobile robots. I also competed at RoboCup with the SocRob team, putting our modules to work on real robotic platforms. The collaboration continued after my internship, and I am now the lead author of a paper we are preparing for IEEE RA-L and IROS.

Software Engineer (JobRad)Mar. 2023 - Nov. 2023

I developed and maintained Odoo ERP workflows, implemented process automation, and supported contract management as part of second-level support. The role strengthened my practical experience with Python, SQL, business processes, and production software maintenance. Sadly I had to leave my team when my internship in Lisbon began

Software Engineer & UI Designer (JW Froehlich)Sep. 2020 - Sep. 2021

I built a .NET/WPF document archiving application from initial requirements analysis through acceptance and company-wide rollout. The project involved stakeholder communication, UI design, SQL integration, and end-to-end delivery. The application remains in daily use, which still makes me rather proud.
